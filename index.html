Know your rights?

Crucial Safeguards in Indian Criminal Law

Right to know clear statutory grounds and reasons for arrest immediately.

Right to consult and be defended by a legal practitioner of choice.

Right to see an official warrant (unless a cognizable offense applies).

Right to be produced before a Magistrate within 24 hours of detention.

Right to receive detailed information regarding bail parameter access.
