Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 11 August 2026

JGals Law Firm ("JGals", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you provide when you visit or use our website https://www.jgals.in/ (the "Website").

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, retain and protect information obtained through the Website and through communications initiated using the Website.

Important: The Website may allow you to provide information relating to a legal dispute, criminal matter, family matter, property dispute, financial matter, cybercrime incident or other legal issue. Please do not submit information that is unnecessary for your initial enquiry.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with the Website, we may collect the following categories of information:

1.1 Information You Provide Directly

When you request a consultation, contact us, submit an enquiry, communicate with us or otherwise interact with the Website, we may collect information such as:

  • Full name
  • Mobile or telephone number
  • Email address
  • Selected legal practice area
  • Information contained in your consultation or enquiry
  • Incident or case briefing information
  • Information you voluntarily provide in messages or communications
  • Documents or other information that you voluntarily send to us

Please avoid providing highly sensitive personal information unless it is reasonably necessary for us to understand or respond to your legal enquiry.

1.2 Automatically Collected Information

When you visit the Website, certain technical information may potentially be collected automatically by the Website, its hosting environment, security systems or other technical services, including:

  • IP address
  • Browser type and version
  • Device type
  • Operating system
  • Pages visited
  • Date and time of access
  • Referring website or URL
  • General website usage information

The exact information collected may depend on the technical configuration of the Website and the services used to operate it.

2. Legal and Case-Related Information

Because JGals Law Firm provides legal services, visitors may voluntarily submit information concerning legal matters.

Such information may include details concerning disputes, complaints, notices, court proceedings, financial matters, property matters, family matters, criminal allegations, cyber incidents, contracts, businesses or other legal circumstances.

We use such information primarily for the purpose for which you provide it, including understanding your enquiry, assessing the nature of your request, communicating with you and determining whether a consultation or legal engagement may be appropriate.

Submission of information through the Website does not by itself create an advocate-client relationship, unless such relationship is separately established in accordance with applicable law and the firm's engagement procedures.

3. How We Use Your Information

We may use personal information for purposes including:

  • Responding to enquiries and consultation requests
  • Contacting you regarding your enquiry
  • Understanding the legal practice area relevant to your request
  • Scheduling or coordinating consultations
  • Providing information about legal services
  • Reviewing information voluntarily submitted by you
  • Communicating regarding existing or potential legal matters
  • Improving the Website and user experience
  • Maintaining Website security
  • Preventing fraud, misuse, spam or unauthorized access
  • Complying with applicable legal and regulatory obligations
  • Maintaining business, administrative and legal records

4. Legal Basis and Consent

Where required by applicable law, we will process personal information based on appropriate legal grounds, including consent, performance of a requested service, compliance with a legal obligation, protection of legitimate interests, or other lawful grounds available under applicable law.

Where you voluntarily submit information through a consultation or contact form, you understand that the information may be used to respond to your request.

5. Communication Through Phone, Email and WhatsApp

The Website provides options to contact JGals Law Firm through telephone, email and WhatsApp.

If you choose to communicate through a third-party communication platform such as WhatsApp, your communication may also be subject to that platform's own privacy policy, security practices and terms of service.

We recommend that you exercise appropriate caution when sending confidential documents or highly sensitive information through third-party communication platforms.

6. Confidentiality of Legal Information

We recognize the importance of confidentiality in legal communications. Information received in connection with legal enquiries and matters will be handled in accordance with applicable professional obligations, applicable law and the firm's internal confidentiality practices.

However, information submitted through a public website, ordinary email, online forms or third-party communication services may not provide the same level of security as dedicated legal communication systems.

Accordingly, please avoid submitting unnecessary confidential information through the Website.

7. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The Website may use cookies or similar technologies for technical functionality, security, performance, analytics or improving the user experience.

Cookies are small files stored on your device by a website. You may be able to control or disable cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect some Website functionality.

If third-party analytics, advertising or other tracking services are implemented on the Website, their collection and processing of data may also be governed by their respective privacy policies.

8. Google Maps and External Services

The Website may contain links or integrations that direct users to external services, including Google Maps, WhatsApp, email services and other third-party websites or platforms.

When you interact with an external service, that service may collect information according to its own privacy policy and terms.

JGals Law Firm does not control the privacy practices of third-party websites or services.

9. Sharing and Disclosure of Personal Information

We do not sell your personal information for monetary consideration.

We may disclose information where reasonably necessary for purposes such as:

  • Responding to your request or providing requested services
  • Communicating with you regarding your matter
  • Using service providers who assist in operating the Website
  • Website hosting, security, maintenance or technical support
  • Email, messaging or communication services
  • Complying with applicable law or legal process
  • Responding to lawful requests from governmental authorities
  • Protecting the rights, property or safety of JGals or others
  • Detecting or preventing fraud, abuse or security incidents

Where third-party service providers process information on our behalf, we will take reasonable steps to ensure that such processing is appropriate for the relevant purpose and consistent with applicable legal requirements.

10. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, including responding to enquiries, providing services, maintaining records, complying with legal obligations and resolving disputes.

Retention periods may vary depending on the nature of the information and the circumstances of the relevant legal matter.

When information is no longer required, we may delete, anonymize or securely dispose of it, subject to applicable legal, professional and record-retention requirements.

11. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, misuse, loss or destruction.

However, no method of transmission over the Internet or method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Therefore, while we strive to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

12. Children's Privacy

The Website is primarily intended for adults seeking legal information or legal services.

We do not knowingly request personal information from children except where such information is lawfully provided by or through a parent, guardian, authorized representative or other person legally entitled to provide it.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us improperly, please contact us so that appropriate action can be considered.

13. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have rights relating to your personal information, including the right to:

  • Request information about personal data held about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of information where legally permissible
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent
  • Request information regarding processing of your personal data
  • Raise a concern regarding the handling of your information

Certain requests may be subject to legal, regulatory, professional or record-retention requirements. We may also need to verify your identity before processing a request.

14. Withdrawal of Consent

Where processing is based on your consent, you may withdraw your consent by contacting us.

Withdrawal of consent will not affect processing that was already carried out lawfully before the withdrawal, and may not affect processing that is permitted or required under another lawful basis.

15. Third-Party Websites

The Website may contain links to third-party websites and services. These may include mapping services, communication platforms, social media platforms, external resources and other websites.

We are not responsible for the privacy policies, content, security or practices of third-party websites.

We recommend reviewing the privacy policy of any third-party website before providing personal information.

16. International Data Transfers

Depending on the hosting infrastructure and third-party services used to operate the Website, personal information may potentially be processed or stored in locations outside India.

Where applicable, such processing will be undertaken in accordance with applicable data protection and privacy requirements.

17. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, services or applicable legal requirements.

When changes are made, the revised Privacy Policy will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.

We encourage you to periodically review this page to remain informed about how we handle personal information.

18. No Legal Advice Through the Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled by the Website. It does not constitute legal advice and does not create an advocate-client relationship.
